Threat Modeling in App Design

What makes an app secure? We must evaluate two things:

  • The Threat: A potential negative action or event (e.g., SQL injection, bad passwords, data theft).
  • The Likelihood: How often or how easily it might happen.
⚠️ Misconception Alert!

Many people think: "If it's in the Apple App Store or Google Play, it is 100% trustworthy."

Reality: While screened for major viruses, many apps legally track excessive data (location, contacts) creating a privacy threat with a high likelihood if users blindly accept permissions.

Listen to the teacher's explanation before moving to the next stage.

Scenario: Designing a password creation screen.
